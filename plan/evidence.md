## Official course evidence

The [5MO101 syllabus](https://www.umu.se/en/education/syllabus/5mo101/),
valid from 18 March 2024, was accessible and checked. It establishes:

- 15 credits, divided equally between animal and plant physiology, with
  module credits 6 + 1.5 + 5 + 2.5.
- Animal structure/function, comparative systems and adaptation; normal
  histology and practical anatomy/physiology.
- Plant organisation, metabolism, regulated growth/development, stress and
  biotechnology, with experimental and communication work.
- Outcomes covering physiological explanation, tissue recognition, experiments,
  scientific reporting, literature evaluation and oral/written communication.
- Prior chemistry/biochemistry and substantial biology, including genetics
  and cell biology; these are historical prerequisites, not safe year-1 assumptions.
- Theory examinations, laboratory reports, individual oral presentation and
  compulsory practical/thematic work.

Its reading list is explicitly unavailable online. Attempts to open
[5MO00S](https://www.umu.se/en/education/syllabus/5mo00s/) and the
[2027 programme syllabus](https://www.umu.se/en/education/syllabus/nglsc-id4272172/)
were unsuccessful. This is an access limitation, not proof of absence.
The user's instruction is to retain the existing core; no content reduction is
inferred merely from its new programme position.

## Books: what was actually checked

Only the public publisher descriptions and tables of contents were inspected,
not full textbook chapters or purchased teaching resources. Their role is to
suggest breadth and organisation. The mapping below proposes **selected
sections within chapter ranges**, not whole-chapter reading assignments.

### Animal reference

Hill, Cavanaugh and Anderson, *Animal Physiology*, fifth edition (2022), print
ISBN **9780197552438**. [Publisher contents](https://www.oup.com.au/books/higher-education/biology/9780197552438)
and [student-resource landing page](https://learninglink.oup.com/access/hill5e-student-resources).

| Proposed unit | Candidate chapter locations |
| --- | --- |
| A1 | 1–2 and 5: organism/environment, cellular foundations and transport |
| A2 | 6–10: nutrition, metabolism, activity and temperature |
| A3 | 22–25: gas exchange, gas transport and circulation |
| A4 | 27–29: water, salts and excretion |
| A5 | 12–17 and 19–20: neural/endocrine regulation, reproduction and movement |

This supports comparative, ecological and evolutionary framing. Specialist
omics and detailed modelling are not needed merely because the book includes
them. Normal-tissue identification still needs the course's specimen manual.

### Plant reference

Taiz, Møller, Murphy and Zieger, *Plant Physiology and Development*, seventh
edition (2022), print ISBN **9780197577240**.
[Publisher contents](https://www.oup.com.au/books/higher-education/biology/9780197577240).

| Proposed unit | Candidate chapter locations |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | 1–3: architecture, walls and genome organisation |
| P2 | 5–8: water relations, minerals and solute transport |
| P3 | 9–14: photosynthesis, carbon movement and metabolism |
| P4 | 4 and 16–23: signalling, development and senescence |
| P5 | 15 and 24: environmental stress and biotic interactions |

The publisher describes an upper-division text. Use it as a selective reference
for the first-year setting, with essential concepts explained in course material.
The predecessor's biotechnology methods and ethical discussion need additional
course material; a table of contents does not establish sufficient coverage.

   practical protocols; plant protocols, experiment durations and equipment.
3. Existing report instructions, marking criteria, thematic topics and papers.
4. Representative theory papers and oral-presentation instructions.
5. Successor syllabus and entry baseline; actual delivery order and elapsed
   time since Intro Computing, if that course precedes physiology.

These are planning dependencies rather than reasons to withhold a provisional
course map. No claim is made that the proposed sequence reproduces an existing
teacher's lesson order.
